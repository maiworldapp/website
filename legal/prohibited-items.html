Prohibited Items

Listings must be legal, safe and appropriate for a local community marketplace.

Privacy Terms Community Rules Prohibited Items Disclaimer Delete Account Contact

Not allowed

  • Illegal goods or services.
  • Weapons, ammunition or explosive items.
  • Illegal drugs or controlled substances.
  • Alcohol or other restricted products where prohibited by MAIWORLD rules.
  • Stolen goods or items the seller does not have the right to offer.
  • Dangerous, unsafe or recalled products.
  • Sexual, exploitative or otherwise prohibited content.
  • Anything that violates applicable law or MAIWORLD community rules.

When in doubt

Do not post the item. MAIWORLD may remove listings or restrict accounts when content breaks platform rules.
