Community Guidelines

Simple rules for keeping MAIWORLD respectful, useful and safe.

Privacy Terms Community Rules Prohibited Items Disclaimer Delete Account Contact

Be respectful

Do not harass, threaten, bully, intimidate or deliberately target other users.

Be honest

Describe listings accurately. Do not impersonate others, mislead users, create fake reviews or use the platform for scams.

Keep communication appropriate

Do not use Rooms, private messages, listings or ratings for abusive, sexual, hateful, threatening or illegal content.

Use safety tools

If something is inappropriate or unsafe, use the available report or block features. Serious immediate danger should be reported to the appropriate local authorities.

Respect local deals

Communicate clearly. If a deal cannot be completed, use the appropriate deal controls instead of misleading the other person.

MAIWORLD is 18+ only. Users must confirm they meet the age requirement before using the platform.
