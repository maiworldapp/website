Delete from inside the app

MAIWORLD includes an account deletion request option under the account and safety settings.

What a deletion request covers

A valid deletion request is intended to remove the MAIWORLD account and associated personal data that MAIWORLD is required to delete, subject to information that may need to be retained for security, legal or fraud-prevention reasons.

External deletion request

MAIWORLD is still in testing. A permanent external deletion request method will be activated on this page before the public Google Play release.

Public release gate: this external request process must be active before MAIWORLD is submitted for public release.
