How MAIWORLD works

Less clutter. Clearer local deals.

The marketplace is designed around a few simple actions so new users can understand what to do without digging through complicated menus.

01

Find a local listing

Browse items available in your local area, including items for sale and items offered free.

02

Show interest

Interested buyers connect through MAIWORLD's deal flow and private messaging when selected.

03

Arrange it directly

Buyer and seller arrange payment and collection themselves. MAIWORLD does not take a commission from the deal.
